THE PRODUCT

About SmartSense.

Practical inventory intelligence for businesses that need to make every shilling count.

OUR PURPOSE

Clarity for everyday commerce.

SmartSense brings stock visibility, sales movement, unit economics and restock planning into one workspace. It is designed to help small commerce businesses make more informed day-to-day decisions.

What you can do

  • Track products, unit costs, selling prices and stock thresholds.
  • Record sales and calculate estimated gross profit from recorded product costs.
  • Review low-stock items and plan proposed purchases against available cash.
  • Export product inventory to CSV.

Version & limitations

SmartSense Demo · Version 1.1. This build stores records locally in your browser. It does not currently provide user accounts, cross-device synchronisation, verified accounting, tax filing or guaranteed demand forecasts. Demo records are illustrative and should be replaced with your own data before business use.

SUPPORT CENTRE

Get started with confidence.

A quick guide to the essential SmartSense workflows.

1. Add your products

Open Inventory, choose Add product, then enter the name, SKU, unit purchase cost, selling price, quantity on hand and minimum stock threshold. Use a unique SKU for each product.

2. Record a sale

Choose Record sale, select a product and quantity, review the total, then confirm. SmartSense reduces the on-hand quantity and adds a transaction to your local sales history.

3. Review stock alerts

Items at or below their minimum stock threshold are marked for attention. A suggested reorder quantity is a planning prompt, not an automatic purchase order.

4. Plan cash before restocking

Enter available cash, upcoming bills, operating expenses and a proposed purchase. The planner estimates remaining cash and flags a shortfall.

5. Export your records

Use Export CSV in Inventory or Settings to save a spreadsheet of your product catalogue. Sales history is not included in the inventory export.

Need assistance?

This demo does not yet include a staffed support desk or cloud account recovery. Keep a copy of your exported records somewhere safe.

LEGAL & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy.

What this SmartSense demo stores and what that means for you.

LAST UPDATED · 12 OCTOBER 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes the current SmartSense demonstration app. It is not a claim that a production-grade privacy programme, account system or cloud sync service is already operating.

2. Information stored

Products, quantities, prices, stock thresholds, locally recorded sales and workspace preferences may be saved in your browser's local storage on the device you use. The demo begins with illustrative sample business records.

3. Where information is stored

In this version, application records are stored locally in your browser and are not automatically synchronised to another device. People with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access local records. Clearing browser data may remove them.

4. Sharing and sale of data

This demo does not provide a built-in feature for selling your inventory or sales records. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details, national identification numbers or other highly sensitive information.

5. Your choices

You can export the product catalogue as CSV. You can reset the demo workspace from Settings, which replaces the locally saved app state with sample data. Save any records you need before resetting.

6. Security and changes

Browser-local storage is not a substitute for an encrypted, access-controlled business database or a verified backup. This policy must be reviewed and updated before a production release that introduces accounts, analytics, third-party services, cloud storage or additional data collection.

7. Contact

For this demonstration, no dedicated privacy contact or support service has been configured. A production release should identify the responsible business and a monitored privacy contact.
